RELIC No.01

Collectible Sculptural Table Light

A contemporary lighting object shaped by the meeting of amber glass, faceted crystal and sculptural bronze.

Amber Glass / Faceted Crystal / Bronze Frame

Download object sheet

A sculptural lighting object inspired by the ritual and warmth of traditional oil lamps. Amber glass, faceted crystal and a flowing bronze frame are brought together in a contemporary collectible form. RELIC No.01 is the first object in the RELIC collection.

  • Glass chimneyAmber-tinted glass
  • Crystal elementFaceted crystal element
  • FrameBronze-toned sculptural metal frame
  • Inner lightCompact inner light holder

  • Total height380 mm
  • Maximum width160 mm
  • Glass chimney180 mm
  • Crystal element60 mm
  • Metal body and base140 mm

Concept dimensions: provisional proportions from the design sheet, not final manufacturing dimensions.

Four elements: an upper glass chimney, an inner light holder, a faceted crystal element and a sculptural metal frame that holds and reveals the inner light. The frame rises from an integrated, flared base that sits directly on the surface. The holder is designed around a compact candle-like light source.
